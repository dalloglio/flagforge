# SRE Playbook

## Review focus

- Reliability, deployability, rollback, configuration, and operational readiness.
- Error handling, health checks, alerts, and runbooks when applicable.
- Platform impact and failure modes.

## Expected inputs

- Technical design, runbook, deployment plan, and validation results.
- Relevant platform ADRs.

## Expected outputs

- Operational risks and mitigations.
- Runbook or alerting requirements.
- Deployment and rollback readiness notes.
